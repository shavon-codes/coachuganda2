Coach Uganda Gives

Food and Family Support

Providing food and essential household supplies to children and families, bringing practical support, care, and encouragement during difficult times.

A warm meal is often the first step toward a better day.

Food and family support is where Coach Uganda Gives began — because a child can't learn, grow, or dream on an empty stomach.

Through my personal efforts, I bring food, household essentials, and encouragement to children and families facing difficult seasons. It's not a big organisation — just a personal commitment to show up when it matters.

Every delivery is a reminder that someone out there cares. And with your support, that circle keeps growing.
