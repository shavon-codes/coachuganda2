Coach Uganda — Child Protection and Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 20th November 2026

1. Introduction

Coach Uganda is an independently managed personal website dedicated to health education, community awareness, and sharing personal efforts to support children, families, and communities in Uganda.

The website features two main categories:

  • Coach Uganda Journal: Publishes educational articles about health, wellness, disease prevention, and healthy living.
  • Coach Uganda Gives: Shares stories, experiences, and personal initiatives focused on supporting vulnerable children, families, and communities in Uganda.

Coach Uganda is a personal initiative and is not a registered non-governmental organization (NGO) or charitable organization.

Protecting the privacy, dignity, safety, and well-being of children and families is an important responsibility of this initiative.

2. Our Commitment to Child Protection

Coach Uganda is committed to treating every child and family featured on this website with dignity, compassion, and respect.

The safety and well-being of children take priority over publicity, storytelling, website traffic, and advertising revenue.

Content published under Coach Uganda Gives is intended to raise awareness, encourage kindness, and share experiences without unnecessarily exposing vulnerable individuals to harm.

3. Protection of Children’s Privacy and Identities

Coach Uganda takes reasonable precautions to protect the identities of children and vulnerable families featured in its articles.

These measures include:

  • Blurring or concealing children’s faces where appropriate.
  • Avoiding the publication of children’s full names.
  • Not revealing private home addresses, school locations, or other sensitive identifying information.
  • Avoiding unnecessary disclosure of personal, medical, or family circumstances.
  • Reviewing photographs and stories before publication to reduce privacy and safety risks.
  • Respecting requests for privacy and confidentiality.

Blurring faces does not always guarantee anonymity, so other identifying details are also considered before publication.

4. Consent for Photographs and Stories

Coach Uganda seeks appropriate permission before publishing identifiable photographs or personal stories involving children and families.

Where children are involved, permission is sought from their parents or legal guardians, with consideration for the children’s wishes and best interests.

Even where consent has been provided, photographs may be blurred or identifying information withheld to provide additional privacy protection.

Consent must be voluntary and informed, and families should understand that published content may be accessible to the public.

5. Respectful Storytelling

Articles published under Coach Uganda Gives aim to promote understanding, compassion, and community support.

Coach Uganda strives to ensure that:

  • Stories are presented truthfully and respectfully.
  • Children and families are not portrayed in degrading or exploitative ways.
  • Personal difficulties are not unnecessarily disclosed to attract attention.
  • Photographs are used responsibly.
  • The dignity of individuals remains a priority.

6. Health Information and Privacy

Articles published under Coach Uganda Journal are intended for general health education and awareness.

The website does not publish identifiable personal medical information about children or families without an appropriate legal basis and necessary permission.

Health articles are provided for informational purposes and are not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

7. Advertising and Website Monetization

Coach Uganda may generate revenue through Google AdSense or other advertising services.

Advertising revenue supports the independent operation of the website and does not automatically represent charitable donations or funds allocated to families.

Advertising and monetization activities do not override Coach Uganda’s commitment to protecting the dignity, safety, and privacy of individuals featured in its articles.

8. Privacy Concerns and Removal Requests

Parents, legal guardians, and individuals featured on Coach Uganda may contact the website operator to raise privacy concerns or request the correction, anonymization, or removal of photographs and personal information.

Requests will be reviewed promptly and handled in accordance with applicable laws and the interests of the individuals concerned.

9. Contact Information

For questions or concerns regarding child protection and privacy, please contact:

Coach Uganda

Website: www.coachuganda.com

Email: info@coachuganda.com

Website Address: https://www.coachuganda.com

Initiative Activities: Uganda

Important Notice: Coach Uganda is an independently managed personal initiative. It is not a registered NGO, charity, or government organization.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 19 November 2026  ·  Last Updated: 19 November 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Coach Uganda.

Coach Uganda is a personal brand, digital content platform, and community-focused initiative dedicated to sharing knowledge, experiences, inspiration, and opportunities through storytelling, educational articles, lifestyle content, and community support.

Our website includes Coach Uganda Journal, Coach Uganda Gives, and information about our services, partnerships, and other activities.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you visit https://www.coachuganda.com/, subscribe to our newsletter, contact us, support our initiatives, or interact with our content and services.

By using our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for particular data processing activities, we will request that consent separately.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information in the following circumstances.

2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you contact us, subscribe to our newsletter, inquire about our services, or participate in our initiatives, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Subject and content of your message
  • Information relating to partnership or service inquiries
  • Information you voluntarily provide when supporting Coach Uganda Gives
  • Other information you choose to share with us

We request that visitors avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific purpose and appropriate safeguards are in place.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising technologies we use may collect technical information, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring websites and navigation activity
  • Approximate geographic location derived from an IP address
  • Cookie identifiers and similar online identifiers

This information may help us maintain website security, understand visitor engagement, improve website performance, and measure the effectiveness of our content.

2.3 Newsletter Information

Visitors may subscribe to receive updates from Coach Uganda Journal and Coach Uganda Gives.

When you subscribe, we may collect your email address and information necessary to manage your subscription.

We use this information to send requested updates, articles, community stories, and relevant announcements.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in those emails or by contacting us directly.

How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  1. Operate, maintain, and improve our website.
  2. Respond to inquiries and communication requests.
  3. Publish and distribute articles through Coach Uganda Journal.
  4. Send newsletters and updates to subscribers.
  5. Coordinate activities associated with Coach Uganda Gives.
  6. Respond to donation and community-support inquiries.
  7. Manage business collaborations, accommodation inquiries, and service requests.
  8. Understand website traffic and audience engagement.
  9. Protect the website against spam, fraud, misuse, and security threats.
  10. Display advertising and measure advertising performance where advertising services are enabled.
  11. Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We process personal information only where we have an appropriate legal basis, such as consent, legitimate interests, contractual necessity, or compliance with legal obligations, as applicable.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, remember preferences, measure website performance, and support advertising.

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device.

Cookies may include:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for website functionality, security, and certain requested services.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used, where enabled, to understand visitor activity and improve website performance.
  • Preference Cookies: Used to remember settings and visitor preferences.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used, where advertising services are enabled, to deliver advertisements, measure their effectiveness, limit repeated advertisements, and, where permitted, support personalized advertising.
  • Third-Party Cookies: May be placed by external services such as advertising providers, analytics platforms, embedded video providers, or social media services.

Where required by applicable law, we will request your consent before activating non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies.

You may also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions.

Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

Coach Uganda may monetize its website through Google AdSense, Google-certified advertising networks, or other advertising partners.

If these services are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a visitor’s previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on users’ visits to our website and other websites on the Internet.

Depending on applicable law, visitor preferences, and our advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Visitors may manage Google advertising personalization through: https://myadcenter.google.com/

Visitors may also learn about opting out of certain third-party interest-based advertising through: https://www.aboutads.info/

For more information about how Google uses information from websites and applications using its services, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where other advertising networks are used, we will provide relevant information about those providers and available privacy choices.

We do not control all data processing performed independently by third-party advertising providers.

Google Analytics and Website Performance

We may use Google Analytics or other website analytics services to understand how visitors interact with Coach Uganda.

If enabled, these services may collect information about:

  • Pages visited
  • Website navigation
  • Device and browser characteristics
  • Approximate location
  • Visitor engagement
  • Website performance

We use analytics information to improve content, usability, and website performance.

Where legally required, analytics technologies will operate only after appropriate visitor consent.

For more information about Google’s privacy practices, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Coach Uganda Journal

Coach Uganda Journal publishes articles and educational content covering topics such as health and wellness, food, home remedies, travel, lifestyle, personal development, and cultural experiences.

Visitors may read publicly available articles without creating an account unless a particular feature states otherwise.

When visitors subscribe to updates, submit inquiries, or interact with additional features, we may process the information necessary to provide those services.

We may use aggregated or appropriately anonymized audience statistics to understand which articles interest readers.

We do not intentionally use sensitive health information disclosed by visitors to create advertising profiles.

Content published in Coach Uganda Journal is intended for general educational and informational purposes and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical, financial, or legal advice.

Coach Uganda Gives and Community Support

Coach Uganda Gives supports community-focused activities involving children, families, education, nutrition, clothing, household essentials, and opportunities for personal development.

Visitors may choose to support these activities through voluntary contributions or other forms of participation.

Depending on the activity, we may collect information necessary to:

  • Respond to supporter inquiries
  • Coordinate voluntary contributions
  • Communicate with community partners
  • Organize support activities
  • Maintain appropriate contribution records
  • Meet applicable legal and accounting obligations

Where payment services are used, transactions may be processed through independent providers such as PayPal, Airtel Money, or MTN Mobile Money.

These providers may process personal and transaction information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not request visitors to submit payment PINs, passwords, or other confidential payment credentials through our website.

We may publish stories, photographs, or videos relating to community activities. We seek appropriate permissions before publishing identifiable information or images, with particular care for children and vulnerable individuals.

We do not intentionally disclose sensitive personal information about beneficiaries without an appropriate lawful basis.

Children’s Privacy

Coach Uganda is committed to protecting children’s privacy.

Although Coach Uganda Gives may feature educational and community initiatives supporting children, our website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through website forms without appropriate parental or guardian authorization.

Where higher age thresholds or additional protections are required by applicable law, we will follow those requirements.

We do not knowingly use children’s personal information for behavioral advertising.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, they may contact us at info@coachuganda.com to request review or deletion.

Our website may contain links to external websites and social media platforms, including:

  • YouTube

These third-party platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

We may share information with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our website and provide requested services.

These may include:

  • Website hosting and technical support providers
  • Email and newsletter service providers
  • Website analytics providers

  • Security and spam-prevention providers

Advertising-related data processing may be treated as sharing or sale under certain privacy laws. Where applicable, we will provide the disclosures, consent mechanisms, and opt-out rights required by those laws.

We require service providers acting on our behalf to handle personal information appropriately and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

These measures may include secure website connections, access controls, software maintenance, and appropriate safeguards for information stored by our service providers.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Visitors should avoid sending passwords, financial PINs, or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary website contact forms.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

Retention periods depend on the nature of the information, the purpose of processing, legal requirements, and the need to maintain appropriate records.

Newsletter subscription information may be retained until a visitor unsubscribes or requests deletion, subject to any necessary suppression records.

Contact inquiries and contribution records are retained only as long as necessary for legitimate operational, legal, or accounting purposes.

The application of particular laws depends on the nature of our activities, the individuals concerned, and the relevant legal jurisdiction.
