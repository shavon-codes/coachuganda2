WHO I AM

About Me

Hi, I'm Coach Uganda

I was born on 2 May 1995 in Jinja, Uganda, and my journey has taken me from Uganda to Germany and across many parts of the world — shaped by education, professional football, business, social care, travel, and my passion for connecting with people.

Today I work as a caregiver in Germany, supporting people in their daily lives, while building businesses, hosting guests, and creating content as Coach Uganda. Every service I offer is built on integrity, warmth, and a genuine desire to serve.

Read More

I began my education at Kirinya Church of Uganda and later attended London High School, Namagabi Secondary School, Nakifuma Secondary School, and Green Valley High School and Busunju High School. I went on to study Education Administration at Kampala International University and Stanford University, gaining the knowledge, discipline, and leadership skills that shape my work today.

Football has always been part of my life. I played at competitive and professional club level, representing SC Villa, Soana, Vitoria University, Kyerima Lions, and African Youth Soccer Academy. The game taught me discipline, teamwork, perseverance, leadership, and consistency. In Uganda I also worked for Cooper and Company as a Sales Manager for two years, before moving to Germany, where I completed two years of social care training and a further year of professional development at Franziskuswerk Akademie.

Travel has broadened my perspective — from Italy, France, Poland, Spain, Croatia, the United States, the United Kingdom, Portugal, and Saudi Arabia to many other destinations. As Coach Uganda, I am also a content creator and influencer, connecting with a growing community across Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. My goal is to keep growing, inspiring others, creating opportunities, and using my experiences and platforms to make a positive impact. I am Waiswa Sulaiman. I am Coach Uganda — and this is my journey.

MY MISSION

The Reason Behind It All

Every child deserves chance to — dream, learn, and build a better future.

Through Coach Uganda Journal, I share real stories, journeys, and lessons. Through Coach Uganda Gives, I turn those stories into action — creating opportunities and giving hope where it matters most.

One Child. One Story. One Act Of Kindness At A Time.

— Coach Uganda

Education

A child's dreams shouldn't end because school is out of reach. We support access to school fees, uniforms, books, and learning materials — helping young minds stay in class and keep dreaming.

Nutrition

No child should have to dream on an empty stomach. We provide food support to children and families, bringing nourishment, comfort, and hope for tomorrow.

Opportunity

Every child deserves a chance to discover what they can become. Through mentorship, sports, and practical skills, we help open doors to brighter possibilities.
